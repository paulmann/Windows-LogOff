Windows-LogOff

Log users off, then clean up what they left

PowerShell 7 utility that force-logs-off Windows sessions and sweeps residual processes, with a two-stage model, safety checks and clear exit codes for multi-session servers.

What it does

  • Graceful logoff followed by a residual process sweep
  • Protects the system session and your own
  • Exit codes that scripts can rely on
